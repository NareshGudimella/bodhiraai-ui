# Lead-form page photo credits

Photos for the redesigned lead-form pages (`/contact`, `/book-demo`, `/student-registration`; not
in legacy, design kit: `docs/site-design-kit.md`). None of them is used on the homepage
(`../../home/`) or another page (`../`). All are free to use commercially under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/); attribution is not required but is recorded here.
Files were downloaded at 2400px wide (2026-11-22), checked with `file`, then cropped and compressed
locally (sharp, mozjpeg q78) at 2× their largest display size (the side column is at most ~520px
wide). Each can be replaced from the Site Image Manager (Web Studio → Site Images → folder
`pages/forms`); no legacy image key had the same role.
